What we collect

  • Your sign-in: your Google account's email address and user id, to keep your settings private to you.
  • Accounts you connect: your YouTube channel's id and name; your Facebook Pages' ids and names; your linked Instagram professional accounts' ids and usernames; and the access tokens those platforms issue so we can publish for you.
  • Your settings: the profiles you create (topics, language, schedules, style, hashtags).
  • What we publish: the title and links of each video we post for you, and any error the platform returned.

How we use it

Only to generate videos according to your settings and publish them to the accounts you chose, and to show you what was published. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

StoryByte Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube access is used only to upload videos, set thumbnails, post a first comment on your own videos, and read your channel's name. See also Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Cloud (Firebase). Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256 and are never sent to your browser. Videos are rendered on our servers and deleted after publishing; a copy staged for Instagram is deleted within two hours.

Services we use

Google Firebase and Cloud Run (hosting, storage, processing), Groq (story writing), Cloudflare Workers AI (pictures), Microsoft Edge text-to-speech and Sarvam AI (narration), and the YouTube, Facebook and Instagram APIs (publishing). Story prompts sent to these services contain your profile's topic and language, not your personal details.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect any account from the dashboard: its tokens are deleted immediately.
  • Revoke access at any time at Google account permissions or Facebook's Business Integrations.
  • To delete your account and all its data, email [contact email] from your sign-in address; we delete it within 30 days. Videos already published stay on your channels until you remove them there.
